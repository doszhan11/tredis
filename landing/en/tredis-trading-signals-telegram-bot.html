The idea

Technology watches the market. The trader makes the decisions.

TREDIS is a platform and Telegram bot for traders that saves the time spent on constant chart monitoring. Instead of searching for entry points by hand, the trader receives trading signals in Telegram: trade direction, entry level, stop loss and several targets (TP). It suits people who trade gold (XAUUSD), Bitcoin, forex pairs and the GER40 index and can't sit at a monitor all day.

The signals are based on TradingView indicators. For example, GENESIS ORDER FLOW shows liquidity and the actions of smart money, while Sweep and MSS (market structure shift) setups help to see when price really changes direction. Market data arrives through an API, and TradingView alerts reach our server through a webhook and become a clear Telegram bot message.

Besides signals, we are building a trading education platform. Authors create streams and publish video lessons and articles, and the system protects the content: it tracks devices, shows a watermark with the login and lets admins block access quickly. This way the author of a strategy or course can earn from their knowledge safely.

Next comes an AI assistant in Telegram. A trader describes a strategy or indicator idea in plain text, and the assistant turns it into an indicator or alert, checks the strategy on history and suggests improvements. After that: copyright protection and selling of strategies, indicators and courses, and auto-trading by the trader's own rules, only with their consent and within limits. Trading automation becomes available without Pine Script or programming skills, and TradingView alerts no longer need manual setup.

TREDIS is not investment advice. Trading on financial markets involves the risk of losing capital. Signals and indicators are analysis tools, not a guarantee of results.

Frequently asked questions

Can I try it right now?

Yes. Connect the Telegram bot and choose your markets. The AI assistant, strategy selling and auto-trading are still in development.

No. Alerts are an analysis tool. The decision to trade and responsibility for the result stay with you.

Does the bot trade for me?

No. For now the bot only sends alerts. We plan auto-trading later, only with your consent and within limits.
